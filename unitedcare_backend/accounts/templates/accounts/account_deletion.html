UNITED CARE - Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their UNITED CARE account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Log in to the UNITED CARE app.
  2. Contact support and request account deletion.
  3. Your account will be reviewed and deactivated for deletion processing.

If you cannot access the app, contact support for assistance.

Email: unitedcareselfgroup@gmail.com

Your account may also be deleted through our secure account deletion process supported by the backend service.
