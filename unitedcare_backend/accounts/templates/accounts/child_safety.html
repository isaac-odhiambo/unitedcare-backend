Child Safety Standards

UNITED CARE is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all users.

Our Commitment

  • We do not tolerate any form of abuse, exploitation, or harmful behavior.
  • We strictly prohibit content related to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
  • Any such content or behavior will result in immediate account suspension and reporting to relevant authorities.

Reporting and Enforcement

  • Users can report inappropriate behavior through our support channels.
  • We review all reports and take appropriate action, including account suspension or removal.
